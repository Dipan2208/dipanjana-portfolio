# Tech logo sources

Brand SVGs are stored locally so the Tech Arsenal does not rely on an icon CDN at runtime.

- `python.svg`, `react.svg`, `leaflet.svg`, `framer.svg`, `gsap.svg`, `javascript.svg`, `flask.svg`, `vite.svg`, `render.svg`, `espressif.svg`, `scikit-learn.svg`, `numpy.svg`, `scipy.svg`, `postgresql.svg`, `supabase.svg`, `tmdb.svg`, `html5.svg`, and `css.svg` are from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) and are released under CC0.
- `java.svg` is from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), licensed under MIT; see `DEVICON-LICENSE.txt`.
- `openai.svg` is the [ChatGPT logo from Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ChatGPT_logo.svg), marked public domain.
- `wifi.svg` and `network.svg` are generic protocol illustrations because Wi-Fi CSI and SMTP/DNS do not have standalone brand logos.
